All Your Queries, Answered

Fair questions about how GetSika works, what it costs, and what happens to your data.

Is it really free?

Yes. No fees, no premium tier, no card required. GetSika earns nothing from any lender, which is exactly why you can trust the comparison.

Do you sell my data?

No. Comparisons run without an account and nothing you type is sold or shared. If you save a payoff plan, it is stored for you and nobody else.

Which lenders does it cover?

Any lender. You enter the terms of the offer in front of you, so it works for banks, savings and loans, fintechs, mobile money advances, and the friend who lends at "small interest".

How accurate are the numbers?

As accurate as the terms you enter. GetSika applies standard amortisation and effective-rate maths to exactly what the lender quoted, including the fees.

Is this financial advice?

No. GetSika shows you accurate numbers so you can decide for yourself. For decisions with big consequences, talk to a licensed adviser too.

Can I use it outside Ghana?

The maths works anywhere, and wider African currency support is on the roadmap. Today it is built and worded for Ghanaian borrowing.
