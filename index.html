How the Score Works
Sika's affordability score (0–100) is calculated using two internationally recognised frameworks:
Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
Safe below 36% · Caution below 50% · High risk above 50%. Used by mortgage lenders globally as the primary affordability test.
True Cost of Credit
Amortisation formula: M = P[r(1+r)ⁿ]/[(1+r)ⁿ-1]. Calculates the exact monthly payment, total interest paid, and opportunity cost over the loan term.
Ghana Reference Rate Intelligence
Live GRR data contextualises your loan rate against the Bank of Ghana benchmark: showing you whether your lender is passing on rate cuts.
